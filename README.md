# Challenge-Cup-26

Current stage: Phase 1A — research design and measurement development (drafts for review).

Data collection: NOT STARTED. No survey, interview, task test, or participant recruitment has been conducted or authorized by this project stage.

The current design examines how undergraduates in finance-related fields use generative AI in research-oriented learning tasks, and how collaboration practices relate to information evaluation, method understanding, independent judgment, and observable task performance. No positive effect is presumed. “Information evaluation” and “research judgment” are current terminology candidates; the design is not frozen.

Candidate institutions include Capital University of Economics and Business, Beijing Technology and Business University, and Central University of Finance and Economics. Candidate status is not permission to access or recruit participants. Enterprise materials remain `PENDING_SUPERVISOR_INTENT`; they have not been analyzed or used in this phase.
